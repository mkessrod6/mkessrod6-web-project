Welcome

Welcome to my ICT280 course website. This site is themed around Project Hail Mary, a science fiction novel by Andy Weir. Whether you're a longtime fan or hearing about it for the first time, this site has everything you need to know.

Use the navigation bar above to explore the site. The About the Book page covers the story, the author, awards, and more.


What You'll Find Here

  • A summary and overview of Project Hail Mary
  • Background on author Andy Weir
  • Awards and recognition the book has received
  • Details on the upcoming film adaptation starring Ryan Gosling
  • A full sources list
Quick Facts

  1. Project Hail Mary was published in May 2021
  2. It won the Hugo Award for Best Novel in 2022
  3. It is Andy Weir's third novel
  4. A film adaptation starring Ryan Gosling is now out in theaters and streaming
