Welcome

Welcome to my ICT280 course website. This site currently features two themed pages: one dedicated to Project Hail Mary, a science fiction novel by Andy Weir, and one dedicated to Steel Ball Run, the seventh part of Hirohiko Araki's manga series JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.

Use the navigation bar above to explore the site. Each page covers its respective topic in depth, including characters, story details, and more.


What You'll Find Here

  • A summary and overview of Project Hail Mary
  • Background on author Andy Weir
  • Awards and recognition Project Hail Mary has received
  • Details on the upcoming film adaptation starring Ryan Gosling
  • An overview of Steel Ball Run and its story
  • Character breakdowns for Johnny Joestar, Gyro Zeppeli, and Funny Valentine
  • A guide to the race stages and key Stands from Part 7
Quick Facts

  1. Project Hail Mary was published in May 2021
  2. It won the Hugo Award for Best Novel in 2022
  3. It is Andy Weir's third novel
  4. A film adaptation starring Ryan Gosling is now out in theaters and streaming
  5. Steel Ball Run is Part 7 of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, serialized from 2004 to 2011
  6. It is set during an alternate-history 1890s cross-continental horse race across America
  7. Steel Ball Run is widely considered the best arc in the JoJo series by fans
