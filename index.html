Welcome

Welcome to my ICT280 course website. This site currently features three themed pages: one dedicated to Project Hail Mary, a science fiction novel by Andy Weir; one dedicated to Steel Ball Run, the seventh part of Hirohiko Araki's manga series JoJo's Bizarre Adventure; and one dedicated to Deadlock, Valve's dieselpunk hero shooter currently in early access.

Use the navigation bar above to explore the site. Each page covers its respective topic in depth, including characters, story details, and more.

Quick Facts

  1. Project Hail Mary was published in May 2021
  2. It won the Hugo Award for Best Novel in 2022
  3. It is Andy Weir's third novel
  4. A film adaptation starring Ryan Gosling is now out in theaters and streaming
  5. Steel Ball Run is Part 7 of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, serialized from 2004 to 2011
  6. It is set during an alternate-history 1890s cross-continental horse race across America
  7. Steel Ball Run is widely considered the best arc in the JoJo series by fans
  8. Deadlock is a 6v6 hero shooter developed by Valve Corporation, currently in early access
  9. It blends third-person shooting with MOBA-style lane pushing and an item economy
  10. Despite invite-only access at launch, it quickly became one of Steam's most-played games
