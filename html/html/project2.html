Project Hail Mary
by Andy Weir

About the Book

Project Hail Mary is a 2021 science fiction novel by Andy Weir, the author of The Martian. The story follows Ryland Grace, a lone astronaut who wakes up on a spacecraft with no memory of how he got there or what his mission is. As his memories slowly return, he realizes he may be humanity's last hope for survival.

The novel masterfully blends hard science with heart, humor, and one of the most memorable friendships in recent science fiction. Weir is known for his meticulous research, and the science in this book — from astrophysics to biology — is both fascinating and largely grounded in real concepts.

Why You Should Read It

  • Gripping mystery — Ryland wakes up with no memory, and the reader discovers the plot alongside him
  • Hard science that is actually explained in a fun, accessible way
  • An unexpected friendship that drives the emotional core of the story
  • Humor and wit balanced with genuinely high-stakes tension
  • A satisfying, hopeful ending — rare in modern sci-fi

 		The Author



Andy Weir began his career as a software engineer before becoming a full-time novelist. He first gained fame with The Martian, which was self-published before becoming a major Hollywood film starring Matt Damon.

A film adaptation of Project Hail Mary, starring Ryan Gosling as Ryland Grace, is now out in theaters and streaming.
